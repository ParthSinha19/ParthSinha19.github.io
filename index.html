Research

MSc Dissertation · In Progress

Robustness and Geometry in Low-Rank Multimodal Fusion

Investigates enhanced dominant modality selection and noise suppression in multimodal fusion, using a joint Wasserstein Autoencoder combined with Low-Rank Multimodal Fusion (jWAE-LMF) to isolate cross-modal interactions within a regularised latent space. Built an original backpropagation profiling framework tracing tensor fusion mechanics, providing evidence of cross-modal gradient coupling under modality dropout. Applied Integrated Gradients and feature ablation to evaluate how the model identifies and weights the dominant modality while suppressing noisy signal, validated across three independent datasets: CMU-MOSI, UR-FUNNY, and POM.
