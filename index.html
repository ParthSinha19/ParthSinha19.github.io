Research

MSc dissertation · in progress

jWAE-LMF

Robustness and Geometry in Low-Rank Multimodal Fusion

Investigates dominant modality selection and noise suppression in multimodal fusion, using a joint Wasserstein Autoencoder combined with Low-Rank Multimodal Fusion to isolate cross-modal interactions within a regularised latent space. Includes an original backpropagation profiling framework tracing tensor fusion mechanics, giving evidence of cross-modal gradient coupling under modality dropout. Integrated Gradients and feature ablation evaluate how the model weights the dominant modality while suppressing noisy signal, validated across three independent datasets: CMU-MOSI, UR-FUNNY, and POM.
